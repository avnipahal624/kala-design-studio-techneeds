# KalaAtlas

## What it is

`/kala-atlas` is an interactive map of India's states and union territories paired with a curated, source-linked regional craft directory and live artisan profiles from the Kalakari directory.

It is a discovery surface. It is not a legal map, a GI registry, an authenticity check or a claim that a craft belongs exclusively to one state.

## Boundary data

- File: `public/data/india-states.geojson` (36 features).
- Source dataset: `LGD_States.geojsonl` from India Geodata, release `admin/states`.
- Source URL: https://github.com/yashveeeeeeer/india-geodata/releases/tag/admin/states
- Licence: CC0 1.0 for the release file.
- Upstream references named by the publisher: Local Government Directory (Ministry of Panchayati Raj), Survey of India, Bhuvan and DataMeet.
- Processing: topology-preserving simplification at 0.018° with Shapely, then stored as GeoJSON. No boundary was hand-drawn or generated by an image model.
- Attribution and limits: `public/data/INDIA_STATES_SOURCE.md` and the map footer.

The map is a presentation asset. It is not a survey or legal boundary and may differ from official representations.

## Interaction

- Click or keyboard-select a state; Enter/Space selects and zooms.
- Zoom with the controls or `+`/`-`; pan by dragging or with arrow keys.
- `0` resets the map to Rajasthan and zoom 1.
- Search by state/UT name with an accessible results list.
- Mobile state selector and list of state profiles.
- A loading state, a retryable error state and a no-craft empty state.

## Craft directory

`src/lib/kala-atlas.ts` contains 14 curated entries across Rajasthan, Uttar Pradesh, Punjab, West Bengal, Bihar, Gujarat, Odisha, Tamil Nadu, Jammu & Kashmir, Telangana and Andhra Pradesh.

Each entry records:

- craft tradition and region;
- concise cultural/technical description;
- technique and materials;
- coordinates for the regional marker;
- a conservative note on what the reference does and does not claim;
- source links.

Primary references include the Ministry of Textiles State Compendium of Handicrafts 2025, the Government of India GI reference document, the Uttar Pradesh Art and Craft pages, the V&A Indian embroidery reference and the Philadelphia Museum of Art's Phulkari resource.

Craft cards link to `/crafts` where a matching library entry exists, or to the general craft library when it does not. They do not fabricate a matching library page.

## Live profiles

- `artisanProfiles.state` is an optional structured state field.
- The demo seed fills it from each fictional maker's region, normalising Delhi NCR and Kashmir Valley.
- `artisans.directory` accepts an optional `state` filter and falls back to `serviceRegion` for profiles without the new field.
- The panel shows only profiles returned by the live directory. If none exist, it says so rather than inventing makers.
- Fictional sample profiles retain their `sampleLabel` and a visible "Demo" marker.

## Privacy and honesty

- No exact home addresses or coordinates are shown for artisans.
- The right panel is an editorial reference, not a certificate for an individual object.
- The UI explicitly separates heritage region from manufacturing location in passport-related copy.
- No government, GI, NGO or museum endorsement is implied.

## Remaining limitations

- Craft coverage is intentionally partial; most states show no curated craft entry yet.
- Boundary data is simplified and not authoritative.
- Craft notes need continued editorial review and DoCRA/community review before production use.
- No browser-based visual test was available in this session.
